## PARTIES

This Office Fit-Out Works Contract (the "Contract") is made on 15 September 2026 between:

1. **Halcyon Ridge Holdings, Inc.**, a corporation with its principal office at Unit 1203, Meridian Point Tower, 88 Sampaguita Ridge Avenue, Barangay Tala Verde, City of San Lorenzo del Mar (the "Employer"); and

B. The Contractor submitted a tender dated 18 August 2026 and has represented that it has the skills, licences, personnel and equipment to carry out the Works.

C. The Parties wish to record the terms on which the Contractor will carry out and complete the Works.

## 4. CONTRACTOR'S OBLIGATIONS

4.1 The Contractor shall carry out the Works with due care, skill and diligence, in accordance with good industry practice, applicable laws and the Building Administration rules.

4.2 The Contractor shall appoint a full-time Site Engineer and a Safety Officer who shall be present on Site whenever work is in progress.

4.3 The Contractor shall submit a detailed construction programme to the Project Manager within seven (7) days after the Date of Agreement and shall update it every two weeks.

4.4 The Contractor shall submit a written progress report to the Project Manager every Friday, covering progress against the programme, safety incidents, permit status and key risks.

4.5 Noisy works, including demolition, core drilling and chasing, shall be scheduled outside the Tower's business hours, in accordance with the Building Administration rules and the conditions of the Building Permit.

4.6 Exterior signage on the Level 12 facade shall be installed only upon issuance of the Signage Permit by the City.

4.7 The Contractor shall keep the Site clean, remove debris daily using the service elevator only, and protect common areas, lifts and finishes of the Tower.

## 5. EMPLOYER'S OBLIGATIONS

5.1 The Employer shall give the Contractor access to the Site from the Commencement Date, subject to the Building Administration rules.

5.2 The Employer appoints Ms. Andrea L. Quintos as Project Manager. The Employer may replace the Project Manager by written notice to the Contractor.

5.3 The Employer shall deliver loose furniture and the audio-visual equipment to the Site on dates to be agreed.

5.4 The Employer shall pay the Contractor in accordance with Clause 7.

## 6. CONTRACT PRICE

6.1 The Contract Price is PHP 48,750,000.00 (Forty-Eight Million Seven Hundred Fifty Thousand Pesos), inclusive of value-added tax.

6.2 The Contract Price is a fixed lump sum and is not subject to adjustment except for Variations under Clause 10 and as otherwise expressly provided in this Contract.

6.3 The Contract Price includes all labour, materials, equipment, supervision, temporary works, testing, overhead and profit.

## 7. PAYMENT

7.1 Advance payment: The Employer shall pay an advance payment of ten percent (10%) of the Contract Price within fourteen (14) days after receipt of the Performance Security and an advance payment guarantee of equal value. The advance payment shall be recovered by deduction of ten percent (10%) from each progress payment.

7.2 Progress claims: The Contractor may submit a progress claim on the last working day of each month, supported by a valuation of work completed and certified by the Contractor's Site Engineer.

7.3 The Employer shall pay each certified progress claim within ninety (90) days after receipt of a valid invoice.

7.4 Retention: The Employer shall retain five percent (5%) of each progress payment. Half of the retention shall be released upon Practical Completion and the balance upon expiry of the Defects Liability Period.

7.5 Late payment: Amounts not paid when due shall bear interest at one percent (1%) per month.

## 8. TIME FOR COMPLETION AND PROGRAMME

8.1 The Contractor shall commence the Works on the Commencement Date and shall achieve Practical Completion on or before the Date for Practical Completion.

8.2 The Contractor shall achieve each milestone listed in Annex B by the milestone dates stated in Annex B.

8.3 Practical Completion is achieved when the Works are complete except for minor defects that do not prevent use of the premises, all tests have passed, and the as-built drawings and operation and maintenance manuals have been delivered.

## 9. PERMITS AND APPROVALS

9.1 The Contractor shall obtain, at its own cost, all Permits listed in Part A of Annex C.

9.2 The Employer shall obtain the Permits listed in Part B of Annex C.

9.3 All Permits shall be issued in the name of the Employer, Halcyon Ridge Holdings, Inc., and shall state the Site address exactly as defined in Clause 1.1.

9.4 No part of the Works that requires a Permit shall commence before that Permit has been issued and is valid.

9.5 The Contractor shall provide the Project Manager with a copy of each Permit within three (3) days after issue and shall maintain a permit register on Site.

9.6 The Contractor shall comply with all conditions attached to any Permit.

## 10. VARIATIONS AND CHANGE MANAGEMENT

10.1 The Project Manager may instruct a Variation in writing. No Variation shall be valid unless instructed in writing by the Project Manager.

10.2 Within seven (7) days after receipt of a Variation instruction, the Contractor shall submit a quotation stating the effect on the Contract Price and on the programme.

10.3 Variations shall be valued using the rates in the Contractor's tender where applicable, or otherwise at fair market rates agreed by the Parties.

10.4 The Contractor shall maintain a Variation register and attach it to each progress claim.

## 11. EXTENSION OF TIME

11.1 The Contractor shall be entitled to an extension of the Date for Practical Completion for delay caused by: (a) Variations; (b) acts or omissions of the Employer; (c) Force Majeure; or (d) delay by a public authority in issuing a Permit, provided the Contractor applied for it on time and in full.

11.2 The Contractor shall give written notice of any event likely to cause delay within fourteen (14) days after it becomes aware of the event, with supporting particulars.

## 12. DELAY AND LIQUIDATED DAMAGES

12.1 If the Contractor fails to achieve Practical Completion by the Date for Practical Completion, the Contractor shall pay the Employer liquidated damages at the rate of one-half of one percent (0.5%) of the Contract Price for each calendar day of delay until Practical Completion is achieved.

12.2 The Parties agree that the liquidated damages are a genuine pre-estimate of the Employer's loss and not a penalty.

12.3 The Employer may deduct liquidated damages from any amount due to the Contractor.

## 13. DEFECTS LIABILITY AND WARRANTIES

13.1 The Defects Liability Period is twelve (12) months from the date of Practical Completion.

13.2 The Contractor shall make good any defect notified during the Defects Liability Period within fourteen (14) days after notice, or within twenty-four (24) hours for defects affecting safety or security.

13.3 The Contractor shall procure and assign to the Employer manufacturer warranties for all mechanical, electrical and fire protection equipment, each for not less than the period stated in the Specifications.

## 14. INSURANCE

14.1 The Contractor shall take out and maintain a Contractor's All Risk insurance policy, in the joint names of the Employer and the Contractor, for not less than the full Contract Price, from the Commencement Date until the end of the Defects Liability Period.

14.2 The Contractor shall maintain third-party liability insurance of not less than PHP 20,000,000.00 per occurrence and workers' compensation insurance as required by law, for the same period.

14.3 The Contractor shall provide certificates of insurance to the Project Manager before the Commencement Date.

## 15. PERFORMANCE SECURITY

15.1 The Contractor shall deliver to the Employer, within ten (10) days after the Date of Agreement, a performance bond for ten percent (10%) of the Contract Price issued by a surety acceptable to the Employer.

15.2 The performance bond shall remain valid until 28 February 2027.

15.3 The Employer may call on the performance bond if the Contractor fails to perform any of its obligations under this Contract.

## 16. INDEMNITY

16.1 The Contractor shall indemnify the Employer against all claims, losses and expenses arising from death, personal injury or damage to property caused by the negligence or breach of contract of the Contractor or its subcontractors.

16.2 The Employer shall indemnify the Contractor against claims arising from the negligence of the Employer or its other contractors.

## 17. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

17.1 The total liability of the Contractor under or in connection with this Contract shall not exceed one hundred percent (100%) of the Contract Price, save for liquidated damages under Clause 12, liability for death or personal injury, and liability for fraud.

17.2 Neither Party shall be liable to the other for loss of profit or indirect or consequential loss.

## 18. SUBCONTRACTING

18.1 The Contractor shall not subcontract any part of the Works without the prior written consent of the Employer, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

18.2 The Contractor remains fully responsible for the acts and omissions of its subcontractors.

## 19. SUSPENSION AND TERMINATION

19.1 The Employer may terminate this Contract by giving fourteen (14) days' written notice if the Contractor: (a) abandons the Works; (b) fails to proceed with due diligence after a written warning; (c) becomes insolvent; or (d) persistently fails to comply with safety requirements or Permit conditions.

19.2 The Contractor may terminate this Contract by giving twenty-eight (28) days' written notice if the Employer fails to pay any undisputed amount for more than sixty (60) days after it is due.

19.3 Upon termination, the Contractor shall be paid for work properly executed up to the date of termination, less any amounts due to the Employer.

## 20. FORCE MAJEURE

20.1 Neither Party shall be liable for failure to perform its obligations to the extent caused by events beyond its reasonable control, including typhoon, earthquake, flood, fire, epidemic, war, civil disturbance and acts of government.

20.2 The affected Party shall notify the other Party within seven (7) days after the start of the Force Majeure event and shall use reasonable efforts to mitigate its effects.

20.3 If a Force Majeure event continues for more than ninety (90) days, either Party may terminate this Contract by written notice.

## 21. NOTICES

21.1 All notices shall be in writing and delivered by hand, courier or e-mail to the addresses stated in the Parties section, or to such other address as a Party notifies in writing.

21.2 A notice is deemed received on the day of delivery by hand or courier, or on the next working day after transmission by e-mail.

## 22. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

22.1 The Parties shall first attempt to resolve any dispute by good-faith negotiation between senior representatives within fourteen (14) days after written notice of the dispute.

22.2 If the dispute is not resolved by negotiation, either Party may refer it to mediation. If mediation fails within thirty (30) days, the dispute shall be finally settled by construction arbitration in accordance with the laws of the Philippines.

22.3 The Contractor shall continue to perform the Works during any dispute.

## 23. DURATION AND RENEWAL

23.1 This Contract takes effect on the Date of Agreement and ends upon expiry of the Defects Liability Period and settlement of all accounts.

23.2 This Contract shall not renew automatically. Any further works or maintenance services shall be the subject of a separate written agreement.

## 24. GENERAL

24.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of the Philippines.

24.2 This Contract may be amended only by written agreement signed by both Parties.

24.3 If any provision is held invalid, the remaining provisions shall continue in force.

## ANNEX A - SCOPE OF WORKS

A1. Strip-out and removal of existing ceilings, partitions, carpets and redundant services on Levels 12 and 14.

A2. New gypsum board partitions, glass meeting room fronts and acoustic ceilings.

A3. Electrical works: new lighting, small power, distribution boards, data cabling containment and connection to the Tower's main supply.

A4. Mechanical works: modification of HVAC ductwork, new fan coil units, and exhaust for the pantry.

A5. Plumbing and sanitary works: new pantry sink, water supply and drainage connections.

A6. Fire protection works: relocation and addition of sprinkler heads and modification of the fire detection and alarm system to suit the new layout.

A7. Finishes and joinery: flooring, wall finishes, pantry cabinets, reception counter, and exterior signage on the Level 12 facade.

A8. Testing and commissioning of all services, and delivery of as-built drawings and operation and maintenance manuals.
